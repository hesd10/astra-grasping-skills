# Single-attempt grasp report

The attempt failed. A stable lift with at least 3 cm desk clearance was not
verified. The carton moved during the first lift and slipped onto its side during
the next segment. Subsequent recovery produced repeated edge contact, rotation,
and rolling rather than reliable enclosure. The operator ended the attempt.

- Verified stable hold meeting the requested clearance: **0 seconds**.
- Elapsed from the first recorded session timestamp to controller exit:
  **23 minutes 48 seconds**. Through verified torque removal: approximately
  **24 minutes 11 seconds**. Documentation time is additional.
- Online motor-command decisions: **35**, including probes and recovery. Each
  submitted bounded motor-delta command counts once; local interpolation steps,
  sensor reads, and image views do not. Startup and requested shutdown are
  separate. The full ordered list is in [DECISIONS.md](evidence/DECISIONS.md).
- The right arm received no position or torque writes during the grasp session.
  Its monitored encoder values showed **zero drift** before shutdown.
- After the operator requested release, the motion owner exited. Torque-enable
  readbacks were **zero on all six motors in each arm**, including both grippers.
  The previously disabled head and base were not commanded.
- No motor status fault was recorded. The maximum temperature sample was
  **53 °C**, with brief elevated samples visible in the log. The controller
  monitored existing hardware limits and did not pause for those samples.
  **No temperature-limit or thermal-protection registers were written.**

The final pre-release images show the carton resting on the desk and the open
gripper above it. No obvious robot or desk damage was visible in the available
views; this is not a physical inspection or a guarantee of no carton damage.

## What failed

The controller kept motor writes bounded and preserved the observation arm, but
the online geometry judgments were unreliable. Wrist-image overlap and shallow
side-view contact were accepted too readily as enclosure. Small tracking
shortfalls and low sustained effort did not distinguish secure side contact from
upper-edge contact. The initial contact assessment was therefore insufficient;
it must not be reused as a successful grasp example.

After the slip, corrections repeatedly moved or rolled the carton while fingers
remained in contact. Several commands intended to clear or center the gripper
did not establish free space. Paired joint changes also failed to preserve the
intended height and inclination consistently. This prolonged the attempt and
increased online interventions. A reset to visibly clear space and a stricter
side-enclosure check should have replaced those repeated contact corrections.

No fresh metric reference was established. Pixel separation and raw encoder
movement alone could not certify an absolute 3 cm clearance, even if a grasp had
persisted. No success or metric-height claim is made.

## Cleaned transferable skill

[skill/SKILL.md](skill/SKILL.md) now emphasizes independent confirmation that both
inner finger faces engage opposing side walls below the upper edges, full swept
finger clearance before alignment, and reobservation after object displacement.
It explains why effort and tracking error support but do not establish a grasp,
why paired joint changes need fresh visual verification, and when to withdraw or
stop instead of repeating ambiguous contact corrections.

Reusable code adds a parameterized side-enclosure gate and a torque-release
helper that attempts every explicitly requested motor and verifies readback.
Read-only connection setup explicitly establishes the driver's baud rate and
checks live model responses without loading calibration.

Only the three cleaned files under `skill/` may transfer. They contain general
procedures and generic hardware/safety code, with no run-specific device
assignments, observations, images, counts, poses, coordinates, trajectories,
calibration, or replay data. Generated bytecode was removed. All three files
were manually reviewed; the supplemental source/file audit passed and recorded
their hashes in [skill_audit.json](evidence/skill_audit.json). Offline helper checks
covered release continuation after a failed write, release readback, rejection
of ambiguous enclosure and partial clearance, and evidence-path containment.
